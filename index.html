About me

Audit discipline meets FP&A thinking

I'm a Finance & Accounting professional with experience spanning Big 4 audit, U.S.-based accounting, FP&A, financial reporting, and financial analysis. My experience has given me a strong understanding of both the accounting foundation behind financial results and the analysis needed to support business decisions.

I began my career in Big 4 audit, working with clients across industries. That experience built my foundation in financial statements, GL analysis, reconciliations, U.S. GAAP, internal controls, and analytical procedures, while strengthening my ability to understand financial results, identify inconsistencies, and communicate findings clearly.

I later pursued my Master's in Finance and Financial Analytics to build on that foundation and strengthen my skills in financial modeling, corporate finance, data analytics, and related areas.

Today, I lead month-end and year-end close while also working across budgeting and forecasting, variance analysis, cash flow planning, financial reporting, and KPI analysis.

My interest sits at the intersection of finance, analytics, and technology, using tools like Excel, Power BI, and Power Query to simplify complex data and turn it into clear, decision-ready insights. I enjoy building models, analyzing performance, and finding efficient ways to turn data into insight, while staying curious about how AI and automation can further improve the process.

Building a strong financial foundation

Rather than focusing only on compliance, I learned how businesses operate beneath the surface — reviewing revenue cycles, cash flows, fixed assets, payroll, working capital, and internal controls to understand the drivers behind financial performance.

As my responsibilities grew, I began mentoring junior team members, reviewing workpapers, and helping deliver client work within demanding reporting timelines. The experience sharpened my analytical mindset and strengthened my ability to identify risks, investigate discrepancies, and communicate financial findings with clarity.

This role ultimately shaped how I approach finance today: not just as numbers on a report, but as information that drives business decisions.
