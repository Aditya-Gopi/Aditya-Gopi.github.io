About me

Audit discipline meets FP&A thinking

I started my career at Deloitte in Hyderabad, where I spent nearly three years executing financial statement audits across 300+ engagements in South Africa and New Zealand. That experience gave me a deep foundation in GL analysis, financial controls, and how large organizations report and move money, skills that take most FP&A analysts years to develop on the job.

I brought that foundation to the US to pursue an MS in Finance and Financial Analytics from the University of New Haven (GPA 3.94, December 2025). Once I completed my MS, I started my new position as a Financial Analyst at ThinkSynergy in Hartford, CT, supporting 100+ client GL accounts, building 10+ three-statement financial models & financial reports for businesses ranging $1M–$10M+ in revenue, managing weekly cash flow reporting of $50K–$100K+, and driving variance analysis across $400K–$1M budgets.

My interest sits at the intersection of finance and technology, using tools like Excel, Power BI, and Python to simplify complex data and translate it into clear, decision-ready outputs. I enjoy the problem-solving side of finance: crunching numbers, building models, and finding efficient ways to turn raw data into insight, while staying curious about how AI and automation can further support that process.
