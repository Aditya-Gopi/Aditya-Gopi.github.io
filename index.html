About me

Audit discipline meets FP&A thinking

I started my career at Deloitte in Hyderabad, where three years of audit work across large enterprise clients gave me an unusually strong foundation in financial controls, data integrity, and how organizations actually move money — skills that most FP&A analysts spend years catching up on.

I brought that foundation to North America, completing a contract FP&A engagement at ThinkSynergy in Hartford, CT, and earlier work in healthcare revenue cycle. I now work toward my CPA while pursuing an MS in Finance and Financial Analytics at the University of New Haven.

My interest sits at the intersection of finance and AI — specifically how tools like Power BI, Python, and emerging LLMs can compress the time between raw data and the insight a CFO actually needs.
